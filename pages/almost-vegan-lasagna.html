Almost Vegan Lasagna

Try this delicious vegan lasagna, made with yuca, black beans, and spinach!

Ingredients

  • 3 yuca
  • 1 can pasta sauce
  • 1 box pre-cooked lasagna noodles
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp nutritional yeast
  • 1 can black beans
  • 1 bag spinach

Directions

  1. Peel and chop the yuca into 1 inch cubes. Boil yuca until soft
  2. Blend yuca with nutritional yeast and egg
  3. Mix black beans, spinach, and pasta sauce in a large bowl
  4. Layer noodles, yuca paste, and sauce mix in a baking pan
  5. Cook at 350 for 40 minutes. Let cool and enjoy
